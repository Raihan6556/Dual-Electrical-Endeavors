## Work

Yesterday I took a leap of faith to learn Solidworks. Today I was bombarded with
Much work but I will continue my learning. I do understand why people like
onshape, but I tried it, and it's just not for me. I don't like the idea that
anything you make is automatically public for all other onshape users. Public
domain is only liberating if you can **voluntarily** choose to make the work
public.

Online CAD software is still cool in general though, and I think if FreeCAD
created a local browser version, many students who are limited by their hardware
(such as chromebooks) will enjoy it.

Since FreeCAD is open source, anyone can start the work on a potential web
version. It would be cool to see it happen, but it is a lot of work to support
the browser and the performance might be very low without WebGPU or WebGL.
